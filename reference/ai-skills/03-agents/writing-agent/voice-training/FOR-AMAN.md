# Train with your writing

Ask: "Extract a professional voice guide from these redacted messages. Cite sample IDs for every inference. Separate observations from guesses."

Supply only messages you wrote and still endorse. Include disagreements and routine replies, not just polished public posts. Label the relationship and channel. Keep rejected examples so you can explain what no longer sounds like you.

Export locally, remove credentials and private third-party facts, and split by thread before analysis. Grant read access to the sanitized training split only. Refuse tools and account access for the extractor. Keep held-out originals private.

Review each inferred rule. Give a preferred rewrite when you reject one. Test no-profile, profile-only, examples-only, and combined drafts on held-out briefs. Approve a profile only after reviewing factual fidelity and blind voice comparisons. Remove derived examples and caches when you revoke a source sample.
