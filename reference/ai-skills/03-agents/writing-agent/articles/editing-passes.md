# Article editing passes

1. Argument: write the thesis beside each section. Cut a section that adds no evidence or inference. Check whether the strongest objection changes the scope.
2. Draft: use the approved outline and claim ledger. Keep unverified notes outside the prose.
3. Slop: remove unsupported significance and repeated closers. Keep necessary detail. Use catalogue IDs in the edit log.
4. Voice: compare with the article profile, not email examples. Read aloud. Ask Aman for a replacement sentence where the voice still feels generic.
5. Facts: verify each factual clause after the style passes. Recheck dates, denominators, modal verbs, source status, quotes, and autobiographical authorization.
6. Reader test: give a fresh reader only the draft. Ask them to state its claim, evidence, strongest objection, and resulting decision. Repair missing links in the reasoning.
7. Aman review: approve stance, factual scope, disclosure, and final text. Publication remains outside the drafting permissions.

Do not keep editing for a lower warning count after the draft becomes less accurate. If a voice change alters a claim, restore the supported meaning and ask Aman about the phrasing.
