# Write an article with the agent

Ask: "Interview me for the thesis before outlining. Challenge unsupported claims. Draft only after I approve the argument."

Supply your audience, their current belief, your thesis, why you believe it, and who bears the cost of the current practice. Add sources with limitations, a real example you may disclose, the strongest objection, and what would change your mind. State the publication's length and disclosure policy.

Train this register on your own approved articles and dictated explanations. Do not import a successful author's opinions as yours. Grant access to selected sources and redacted examples. Refuse publication permissions and private interviews without consent.

Approve the thesis before the outline. Check the source ledger before the prose. Run the article-intro and dense-paragraph evals. Ask a target reader to explain the argument without the brief. Correct missing reasoning, then approve the final draft. You retain responsibility for allegations and first-person claims.
