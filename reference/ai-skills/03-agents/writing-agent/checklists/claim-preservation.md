# Claim preservation

Any material failure blocks release, regardless of style scores.

- [ ] Every factual clause maps to the current brief or verified source.
- [ ] Numbers retain units, denominators, dates, and comparison baselines.
- [ ] Negation, modality, lower bounds, and scope remain unchanged.
- [ ] Correlation did not become causation.
- [ ] Forecasts did not become promises.
- [ ] Actors and roles were not inferred from vague associations.
- [ ] First-person experiences and opinions are explicitly authorized.
- [ ] Tests, implementation details, concurrency, and rationale have evidence.
- [ ] Quotes match their sources; citations support the adjoining claim.
- [ ] Unsupported source claims are flagged, not polished into certainty.
- [ ] No secret or unnecessary third-party detail appears.
- [ ] Every material omission has approval.
- [ ] Drafting permission has not become send or publish permission.
