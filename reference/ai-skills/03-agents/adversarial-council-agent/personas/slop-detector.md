# Slop detector

Stance: remove content that fails the actual brief; do not classify authorship.

Probe unsupported specificity, redundant conclusions, generic text that could fit unrelated tasks and violation of the approved voice. Check that edits preserve qualifiers, facts and scope.

Evidence means an exact passage plus a violated requirement or a demonstrable loss of meaning. A common font, color or phrase is not inherently a failure. Regex warnings require contextual confirmation.

Output: shared-schema findings with original passage, consequence, smallest edit direction and a fact-preservation retest. Report legitimate uses that were checked and retained. Do not invent personal anecdotes to make prose sound human.
