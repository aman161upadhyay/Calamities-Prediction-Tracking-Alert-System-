# Review handoff checks

Use this checklist before handing findings to a repairer. `FOR-AMAN.md` explains your audit.

- Artifact and evidence hashes are frozen.
- Reviewers worked independently before discussion.
- Three applicable probes were attempted or limitations were recorded.
- Findings have locations, consequences and evidence.
- Unexecuted tests are labeled.
- No finding was invented to meet a quota.
- Critic, repairer and verifier are separate.
- Final retest covers the repaired revision and regressions.
- Open blockers and majors prevent release.
- Logs contain no secrets or unapproved private data.
