# Writing rubric

Judge against the audience, register, facts and length in the brief. Approved examples demonstrate voice, not current factual truth.

| Criterion | PASS evidence | FAIL example |
|---|---|---|
| Factual fidelity, hard | Every material assertion matches supplied facts or attributable evidence. | A draft changes “considering” into a commitment. |
| Authority and privacy, hard | No invented personal experience, disclosure or unauthorized send. | A private sample supplies an unrelated recipient's personal details. |
| Task and format, hard | Requested action and format are present within the specified limit. | A short reply becomes an essay without the requested answer. |
| Voice, diagnostic | Located phrasing matches the approved register and Aman accepts it. | Ceremonial wording conflicts with the supplied plain-text examples. |
| Economy, diagnostic | Each passage contributes information or a needed social action. | A conclusion repeats the opening without consequence. |

Return criterion, PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN, exact passage and concise evidence. No aggregate score overrides a hard failure. Aman judges voice; an LLM is secondary until calibrated. Preserve qualifiers when proposing edits.
