# Review your additions

Give the coordinator your proposed diff, frozen tree hash, intended use and acceptance checks. Say: “Review this addition under PRINCIPLES.md and the council protocol. Keep first reviews independent. Do not push or edit policy.”

Supply the sources you actually used, their licensing evidence and any gold examples. Name the reviewer who can resolve domain disagreements. Set total dollars, model calls, tool calls and wall time before review; use the agent's pilot budget only after you approve it.

Grant read-only repository and source access. Grant a disposable sandbox for approved reproduction. Refuse secrets, production mutation, unrestricted network and push credentials. Keep the reviewer independent from the repairer.

Run the adversarial agent's clean-control and seeded-defect evals before trusting a new reviewer. Ask the chairman for located findings rather than an approval percentage. Review the evidence yourself for licensing, privacy or high-impact disputes.

After repairs, request an independent retest and a follow-up round. Check zero open blockers, zero open majors and the diminishing-return condition. Commit the sanitized log with your change. If budget ends or evidence is missing, do not push. You can approve more review, but cannot silently treat an incomplete review as accepted.
