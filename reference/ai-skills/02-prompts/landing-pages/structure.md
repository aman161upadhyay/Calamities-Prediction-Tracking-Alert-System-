# Order by visitor uncertainty

1. Name the audience/problem and product in plain language.
2. Show the primary action, useful demonstration and honest friction.
3. Include verified proof if supplied.
4. Connect two to four outcomes to actual product behavior.
5. Explain the workflow.
6. Answer material objections about fit, integration, privacy or migration.
7. State plan/trial terms when relevant.
8. Repeat the same primary CTA and provide legitimate footer links.

An event may lead with date and location. A developer tool may need code before proof. A high-cost service may need qualification first.

This is an authored pattern from research 05, not a proven conversion formula. The landing-guide supplements had limited full-page verification. Test task completion and business outcomes separately.
