# Supply the offer before the page

Give the agent the buyer, primary alternative, mechanism, CTA, real terms and approved proof. Include rights-cleared imagery and three references with specific features to borrow.

Ask for a token/hierarchy plan before implementation. Grant preview and branch access. Refuse fabricated logos, conversion claims and false urgency. Require your approval for publication or live forms. Test the CTA and failure recovery on desktop and mobile, then review claims with your writing agent.
