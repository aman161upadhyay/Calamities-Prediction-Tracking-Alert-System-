# Keep claims traceable

Use the [writing agent](../../03-agents/writing-agent/) for approved voice and editing. Give it the proof ledger, not just a mood word.

Name buyer, task, mechanism and next action. Keep one primary CTA consistent. Explain signup, payment and trial friction before asking for commitment.

Every customer quote, metric and integration claim needs source, date, permission and supported scope. Omit unsupported proof. Keep questions for Aman outside publishable copy.

Describe errors with recovery steps. State billing units and cancellation. Do not invent scarcity or compliance. A copy framework organizes evidence; it does not establish conversion.
